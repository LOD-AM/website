Welcome to Linked Open Data for Ancient Metallurgy

Hi, I'm Fabian. Welcome to my digital sandbox, where I play with Linked Open Data for Ancient Metallurgy (LOD-AM)—a little scientific side hustle of mine.

This is my private experiment, a one-man show where I get to ask questions about copper-based metallurgy in Eurasia and all the Research Data Management (RDM) chaos that comes with it.
