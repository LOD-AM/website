Analyses in Hauptmann et al. 2002

Chemical composition and lead isotopy of metal objects from the "Royal" Tomb and other related finds at Arslantepe, Eastern Anatolia

The values are those reported in the publication, one row per analysis. Each row links to the analysis and to the analysed object. Nothing has been calculated, converted or rounded.

Distribution of element contents

One histogram per element: the number of analyses per range of reported values (wt %), on a logarithmic axis (four ranges per power of ten), the same axis for all elements. The count axis differs per element. Values reported as “not detected”, below a detection limit (“< x”) or as 0 cannot be shown on a logarithmic axis and are counted below each chart. The values are as reported; ranges and counts are only for the chart. Point at a bar for its range and count. P and Te are not shown here; their values are in the table below.
