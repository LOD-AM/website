Data

LOD-AM publishes archaeometallurgical data on copper-base objects from Eurasia as Linked Open Data. The data are compiled from published sources and comprise the objects, their archaeological contexts and dating, and the chemical and lead isotope analyses reported for them.

Scope of the released data

The data are released one publication at a time. A release comprises the finds and analyses reported in that publication, together with the sites, contexts, phases, typological classes, laboratories and bibliographic records to which they refer. The underlying research database contains further sites and publications, which will be released successively. The present selection is therefore neither complete nor representative, and it should not be used for quantitative comparisons beyond the publications listed below.

Presentation of values

  • All values are reproduced as reported in the source publication, including values such as 0 or 0.0001. No values have been recalculated, normalised, rounded or corrected, and none are given that the source does not report.
  • Elements reported as not detected are recorded as such and are distinguished from elements that were not analysed. Detection limits are given only where the source reports them (“< x”).
  • Copper values of copper-base objects are not included.
  • A find is dated by its archaeological context (feature or site), not individually.

The data model follows CIDOC CRM 7.1.3 with the extensions CRMsci and CRMarchaeo; analytical results are expressed with the Allotrope Foundation Ontologies and bibliographic records with FaBiO. Object, site and feature types are linked to the Getty Art & Architecture Thesaurus. The modelling rules are documented in the LOD-AM conventions. Every record has a persistent IRI under https://lod-am.net/id/ and can be downloaded in Turtle and JSON-LD from its page.

Licence and citation

The data are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). When using them, please cite the individual records (a citation is given on each record page) or the dataset, and the source publications. Versioned releases with a DOI are in preparation.

Corrections

Errors in the data, whether in transcription, attribution or modelling, can be reported to info@lod-am.net. Please name the record (its IRI) and, where possible, the page or table of the source publication. Corrections are listed in the changelog below.

Map data

The site maps show the relief and the present-day water network, without borders, settlements, roads or modern reservoirs. Every site is shown on the same map of the whole area of the research database (21–62° E, 30–53° N). Rivers, natural lakes and coastlines are shown as they are today, not as they were in antiquity.
