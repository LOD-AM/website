Data

LOD-AM publishes its research data as Linked Open Data. Every record has its own address under https://lod-am.net/id/; this page lists what is released so far. The data is published one publication at a time.

How the data is modelled is documented in the LOD-AM conventions (CIDOC CRM 7.1.3, Allotrope, FaBiO, Getty AAT).
