Privacy Policy

This page explains which personal data is processed when you use lod-am.net, why, on which legal basis, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR is:

Fabian Fricke
Geranienstraße 6
12203 Berlin
Germany
Email: info@lod-am.net

LOD-AM is a private, non-commercial project. No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so.

2. Hosting

This website is hosted on the controller's own hardware in Germany. No external hosting provider, content delivery network or tracking service is involved. The domain is registered with STRATO GmbH, Berlin, which also provides the name service (DNS) for it.

3. Visiting this website: server log files

Whenever you open a page, your browser automatically sends data to the server, which the reverse proxy stores in log files:

  • your IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • the requested page or file, the HTTP method and status code, and the amount of data transferred
  • the website you came from (referrer), if your browser sends it
  • browser type and version, and operating system (user agent)

Purpose: delivering the website and ensuring its technical operation and security, in particular detecting and defending against attacks and misuse, and analysing errors.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in the purposes above. Without this data, the website cannot be delivered.

Storage period: the log files are rotated weekly and deleted automatically after five weeks at the latest. Log data is only kept longer if it is needed to investigate a specific security incident, and only until that is resolved.

Recipients: none. The log data is not passed on and is not combined with other data sources.

4. SPARQL query editor and endpoint (triple.lod-am.net), site search

The query editor at lod-am.net/query/ and the public SPARQL endpoint https://triple.lod-am.net/LOD-AM/sparql are operated by the controller on the same hardware as this website (section 2). Opening the editor sends nothing to triple.lod-am.net. Only when you run a query (by clicking "Run", or by sending a query to the endpoint directly) does your browser connect to triple.lod-am.net and transmit:

  • your IP address, date and time, the requested address, HTTP method, status code, amount of data transferred, referrer and user agent. The reverse proxy stores these in its log files, as described in section 3;
  • the text of your query. The triplestore software (Apache Jena Fuseki) writes it, with date and time, to its own log. This log does not contain your IP address.

Purpose: answering your query, and ensuring the technical operation and security of the endpoint, in particular detecting and defending against misuse and overload, and analysing errors.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in the purposes above. Without this data, queries cannot be answered.

Storage period: the proxy log files as described in section 3. The triplestore log is limited in size: older entries are deleted automatically once it exceeds 50 MB, and the whole log when the software is updated or restarted.

Recipients: none.

The query editor does not store anything on your device; your query is lost when you leave the page. If you open an example query, the query is part of the page address (lod-am.net/query/?query=…) and therefore appears in the log files described in section 3. The same applies to search terms entered in the search field of the navigation bar (lod-am.net/search/?q=…). The search itself runs in your browser on files from lod-am.net and contacts no other server.

5. Cookies and storage on your device

This website itself does not set cookies. There is no tracking, no web analytics and no advertising.

If you switch between light and dark mode with the button in the navigation bar, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (key "lodam-theme"), so that the following pages use it. Each page reads this value when it loads. It is not sent to the server or to anyone else. This storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can remove it at any time by deleting the website data for lod-am.net in your browser.

Only if you load the comments and sign in with GitHub there (see section 6), giscus stores a login token in your browser's local storage. This happens on the basis of your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by signing in. You can remove it at any time by signing out in the comment area or deleting the website data in your browser.

6. Comments (giscus and GitHub)

Blog posts offer comments through giscus, an open-source comment system that stores comments as GitHub Discussions in the public repository LOD-AM/website.

Nothing is loaded without your action. The comment area is only loaded when you click "Load comments" below a post. Until then, your browser makes no connection to giscus or GitHub.

After the click, your browser connects to the servers of giscus.app and GitHub. They receive the technical data needed for this, in particular your IP address, the address of the page and your user agent. Existing comments are displayed. To write a comment or a reaction, you need a GitHub account and must sign in to GitHub via giscus. Your comment is then published in the GitHub discussion under your GitHub username and is publicly visible.

  • Providers: the giscus organization, operator of giscus.app (privacy policy); GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (privacy statement).
  • Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by clicking "Load comments". Writing a comment is subject to GitHub's terms, which you accept with your GitHub account.
  • Transfer to the USA: GitHub, Inc. is located in the USA, and the servers of giscus.app may also be located outside the EU. GitHub, Inc. is not certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so no adequacy decision of the European Commission covers this transfer. It therefore takes place only on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by clicking "Load comments" after being informed about it next to the button. Possible risks: the USA does not provide a level of data protection equivalent to that of the EU. In particular, US authorities may be able to access the data without you being informed, and you may not have effective legal remedies against this.
  • Storage period: comments remain in the GitHub discussion until you or the controller delete them. You can edit or delete your own comments on GitHub at any time. Other data processed by giscus.app and GitHub is governed by their privacy policies.
  • Withdrawal: you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Simply don't load the comments again; when you reload the page, they are not loaded until you click again. Existing comments can be deleted on GitHub, or ask the controller to delete them.

7. Contact by email

If you write to info@lod-am.net, your email address, the time and the content of your message are processed in order to answer you.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries), or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry concerns a contract or pre-contractual measures.
  • Recipient: the mailbox is hosted by STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The data is processed in Germany.
  • Storage period: emails are deleted once the matter is settled, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

8. Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, for example GitHub, Mastodon, DOI and publisher pages. No data is transmitted to these sites until you click a link. After that, the privacy policy of the respective site applies.

9. Data security

All connections to this website are encrypted with TLS (HTTPS). The server software is kept up to date, and administrative access is restricted and protected by authentication.

10. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • withdrawal of consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR); this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal

To exercise your rights, an informal email to info@lod-am.net is sufficient.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (server log files, email contact), you have the right to object to it at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. The controller will then no longer process the data, unless there are compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. The authority responsible for the controller is:

Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit

www.datenschutz-berlin.de

11. Further information

You are not obliged to provide personal data. Visiting the website technically requires the log data described in section 3; queries, comments and emails are voluntary. No automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

This privacy policy is updated when the website or the legal situation changes. The current version is always available on this page.
